The Chef

A Passion for Perfection, A Legacy of Flavour

Chef Oby Rosa is a culinary artist with over 24 years of experience crafting extraordinary dining experiences across South Florida and beyond. His journey began in the finest kitchens of Latin America, where he mastered the art of balancing bold tradition with refined technique.

Specialising in private dining and luxury events, Chef Oby Rosa's culinary philosophy is simple: every meal is a once-in-a-lifetime memory. His command of international cuisines, from Mediterranean to Pan-Latin, combined with certified kosher expertise, makes him the preferred choice for South Florida's most exclusive clientele.
